Job Title: Senior Data Scientist
Company: Retail Intelligence Analytics
Location: Chicago, IL / Remote

About the Role:
Join our analytics core team to transform terabytes of customer transactions into predictive intelligence, recommendation engines, and strategic growth insights.

Key Responsibilities:
- Extract, clean, and explore large-scale tabular data using SQL and Pandas.
- Build statistical models, hypothesis testing frameworks, and A/B test experiments.
- Develop predictive machine learning models for customer lifetime value and churn prevention.
- Create interactive executive dashboards using Tableau and Power BI.
- Communicate complex analytical insights to executive stakeholders.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, or Computer Science.
- 3+ years of applied experience in Data Science and quantitative modeling.
- Advanced proficiency in Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn.
- Strong grounding in Statistics, A/B testing, and exploratory data analysis.
- Experience building interactive dashboards with Tableau.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Familiarity with Cloud platforms like Google Cloud Platform or AWS.
- Experience with NLP and text processing using NLTK or spaCy.
- Exposure to BigQuery and Snowflake.
